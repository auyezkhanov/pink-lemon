About the studio

A small studio that thinks like an in-house team

Pink Lemon was founded on a simple idea: brands don't need more noise, they need more clarity. We're a compact team of strategists, designers and writers who get close enough to a brand to make decisions like it's our own.

Our story

Built from client-side frustration

Our founders spent years inside in-house marketing teams, watching good strategy get lost in disconnected execution — a brand deck nobody referenced, a website that didn't match the pitch. Pink Lemon exists to close that gap: one team, one point of view, from positioning through to the final pixel.

Today we work with a deliberately small roster of clients at a time, so every project gets senior attention from strategy to delivery — not just at kickoff.

What we believe

The principles behind the work

Clarity first

If a strategy can't be explained in one sentence, it isn't ready to be designed yet.

Craft over trend

We chase what's right for the brand, not what's popular in this year's design feeds.

Real collaboration

The best work happens in the room with the client, not delivered over a call at the end.

Courage to simplify

Cutting the unnecessary is often the hardest — and most valuable — part of the job.
